## Isolation and limits

Lunar actors are separate showcase identities, never MinimalAnimal/Species/CreativeAsset, and cannot sell, trade, breed, earn, persist, or return home. The former two ordinary stand-ins and six placeholder circles are removed; there is no fabricated sixth actor. Other three biome rosters and their isolated boarding demo remain unchanged. Lunar boarding rejects explicitly.

Pause/blur/dialog/hidden stops active-time motion; leaving destroys the private visit scene and cancels late loads. Re-entry starts fresh. Root motion stays in place except explicit temporary lift/lower. Existing authored humanoid sleeve surfaces are fused and gestures remain restrained; no fingers, facial morphs or full cloth reconstruction. Mobile/GPU performance and browser visual acceptance remain unmeasured.

Entry: Friends → 月球剧场 → click a character or portrait → action. Chicken offers dribble/shoot/celebrate, not carry. The other four offer greeting, individual gesture, and temporary carry when nearby. Stop is explicit; automatic performances are finite.
