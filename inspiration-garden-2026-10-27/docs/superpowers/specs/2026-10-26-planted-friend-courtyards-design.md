Root-approved short design, 2026-10-26. User requested richer planted gardens and actual paths/boundaries while retaining the approved architecture. Root owns public publication and browser QA.

## Approach and limits
Use the existing authored composition/edge system, not new scenery-only filler or a navigation rewrite. Repeating low coherent plants into grouped beds is preferable to scattering unrelated species. Modest land extensions preserve all approved building coordinates, footprints, styles and counts. Existing collector identities, economy, saves, five palettes, two-step entry zoom and outside plant interaction stay unchanged.

All plants remain real 1×1 growable/interactive catalogue species (Kelly's existing 2×2 piano canopy remains). New art is staged by a separate worker and promoted only with its metadata contract. No new large plant or architecture is introduced. Every empty support must remain connected with plants/buildings treated as occupied; every plant/building needs a reachable empty exterior bay. At least 55% of each new island remains genuinely empty, and no decorative edge crosses the central spine or closes a building/plant approach.

## Alternatives considered
Adding plants to current few land cells would squeeze interaction bays and still leave empty paved islands. Fully rebuilding islands would violate the approved architecture. The chosen small extensions and grouped planting preserve the accepted landmarks and turn leftover space into legible gardens.
