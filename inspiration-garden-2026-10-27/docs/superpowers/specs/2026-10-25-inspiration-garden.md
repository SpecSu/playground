## Experience
Mobile-first 2D/2.5D miniature illustrated island. Beautiful transparent plant/character images, layered terrain and water, subtle local animation. Fixed isometric view with pan/zoom, NOT arbitrary 3D rotation. Four land cells initially. Tap destinations for avatar movement with brief animation, no long compulsory navigation. Familiar buy/plant/wait/reveal/play/sell/expand loop. Mature plants can remain as interactive residents OR be sold whole for coins, freeing their cell. No separate fruit system in this MVP.

Coins fund seeds and construction; crystals only fund creation, never refunded by sale. Same seed yields same variety/asset and interaction, local cosmetic variation optional. First basic plant 15 seconds; subsequent growth 45–120 seconds as catalogue data. Starting 120 coins, 3 crystals and 2 radish seeds. Daily claim grants 2 crystals once per Asia/Shanghai day (demo balance, adjustable). Buying crystals is clearly labeled simulated demo grant, no checkout or real charge. No negative balances/double spending from repeated clicks. Time and save restoration tested.

8 basic seed types, initially radish and tomato; unlock others progressively by completed sales, unknown names not exposed in advance. Base seeds cost 8–24 coins and mature whole-plant sale returns a modest positive margin. New creative varieties use a bounded economic template; unusual art gives no unlimited economic advantage.

Creation consumes 1 crystal once confirmed and successfully creates a new variety record + one seed. Two entry paths: select parent variety and remix; or invent without parent. Text/choice input chooses among OFFLINE authored creative samples, never claim live AI. Prominent but gentle '离线创意样例' disclosure in creation sheet. No upload of personal photos, no API keys. Give suitable idea chips and clearly explain samples match supported inspiration directions; don't pretend arbitrary prompts are fully realized. Reveal novelty at maturation, but describe basic interaction and costs before commitment where appropriate. Cancel preview consumes nothing. Existing owned variety seeds can be bought again with coins. Friend-acquired UGC seeds come from visits, not global store unless previously personally created.

At least 8 visually distinct creative plants, each with readable tactile/sonic/personality interaction. Suggested roles: drum pumpkin; singing/bell eggplant; puffer tomato; whisper radish; keyboard corn; wave cabbage; sleepy sunflower; glow/shadow mushroom. At least 2 original invented species examples (not only recolors). Every sprite-based interaction locally executed, audio unlocked by user gesture and mute available. No multi-plant ensemble, freeform chat, microphone or camera.

Three distinct clearly labeled demonstration gardens (not fake real users), with different shapes and plant collections. Visit → interact with mature plant → receive one seed of that variety. Per local visitor + variety + Asia/Shanghai day limit 1, shared across copies/gardens (parent MVP ruling, not final economic policy). Seed can be replanted and spread onward; original author ID/name persists. Remix creates a new author record with parent/root provenance. Tests prove a second-generation copy preserves original attribution. Local-only saves and simulated gardens clearly disclosed; no actual multiplayer claim.

## Construction kit
Exactly four basic kinds: plantable land, nonplantable wood platform, edge-mounted wood fence, edge-mounted low stone wall. Land/platform snap to connected grid; fences/walls snap along cell edges and don't consume a planting cell. Build, move, reclaim with explicit coins, existing plants prevent removing/moving occupied supporting land. Prevent orphan/disconnected island and inaccessible avatar spawn. Decorative fences should not introduce pathfinding deadlocks; treat as low passable visual edges for this MVP, communicate if needed. Future crystal-authored construction plans are NOT implemented, no dead button implying available feature. Starting two-by-two land, expandable island with differing outlines. At most 36 tiles for performance; don't expose engineering jargon in UI.
